# Non-Row Constructive

## Scope

Search for constructive layout families that are not simple row-based or standard boundary-frame arrangements.

## Bias

Favor asymmetric, clustered, corridor-like, mixed interior-frame, or other deliberately non-row constructive ideas.

## Deprioritize

- small polishing steps on an existing row-like incumbent
- purely local repair unless it directly supports a new constructive family

## Strong Signals

- repeated emergence of viable non-row macro structure
- competitive probe/verify signal from asymmetric or clustered seeds
- layouts that occupy boundary and interior differently from the incumbent family

## Representative Moves

- cluster-first constructive seeds
- corridor or scaffold constructions
- asymmetric boundary usage
- mixed interior / boundary skeletons

## Cross-Direction Snapshot Use

Treat other directions' snapshots as structural comparisons or controls only. Keep the main search centered on genuinely non-row constructive families, and do not switch to another direction's incumbent as the working candidate.
